▤   How It Works

Least count = 0.05 mm. Observed reading = main scale reading + (coinciding vernier division × 0.05 mm). Corrected reading = observed reading − zero error. Apply Zero Error to shift the main-scale zero against the vernier zero on the 3D calliper and update the reading (type a value or use the slider). Drag the instrument to rotate it; Jaw View focuses on the jaws.
